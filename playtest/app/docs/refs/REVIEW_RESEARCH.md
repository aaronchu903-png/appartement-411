# Review research notes for Le Jardin (2026-10-15, Croissant)
Evidence = peer-reviewed / published research. Practice = what apps do (not proof it works).

## Research (evidence)
- Testing effect: retrieval beats restudy at 2 days / 1 week, though restudy looks better at 5 min and inflates confidence. Roediger & Karpicke 2006, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16507066/ -> self-rated "remembered" invites false confidence; require an answer.
- Desirable difficulties: spacing, interleaving, varied presentation, tests as learning events. R. Bjork, https://bjorklab.psych.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2016/07/RBjork_inpress.pdf ; Bjork & Bjork 2011, https://bjorklab.psych.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2016/04/EBjork_RBjork_2011.pdf -> mix exercise types, make tests progressively harder.
- Expanding retrieval (tests made progressively harder) helps long-term recall: Landauer & Bjork 1978 as cited in Bjork 1994, https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/spaced-repetition/1994-bjork.pdf
- Duolingo HLR (Settles & Meeder 2016): grading from correctness of answers in exercises feeds a forgetting model; harder words = irregular forms, imparfait etc. https://blog.duolingo.com/how-we-learn-how-you-learn/
- Recognition -> recall ordering: supported indirectly (above); I did not open a dedicated recognition-vs-recall vocab study, so treat stage order as reasonable practice, not proven.

## App practice
- Duolingo: Birdbrain predicts P(correct) per learner x exercise and picks "challenging but attainable" exercises; labelling hard exercises raised engagement. https://blog.duolingo.com/learning-how-to-help-you-learn-introducing-birdbrain/ , https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-difficult-exercises/
- Clozemaster: sentence with one gap, multiple choice or typing; any wrong resets to 0%. Users: MC for new, typing for reviews. https://docs.clozemaster.com/article/37-how-do-i-master-something , https://www.clozemaster.com/
- Memrise: review mixes multiple choice, tapping scrambled words, typing, listening; mistakes return soon. https://memrise.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360015973818-How-many-testing-types-are-there (seen via search snippet)
- Language Reactor PhrasePump: listening-first sentence review, cloze-style recall, but still OK/Forgot self-rating; scheduled with Ebisu. https://www.reddit.com/r/German/comments/yh4svg/ , https://forum.languagelearningwithnetflix.com/t/i-changed-my-mind-on-phrasepump-its-amazing/33176
- 百词斩: must choose correct meaning (with picture) before seeing details; deep review = 英文选义/中文选词/听音选义/填空拼写/组合拼写/全拼默写. https://www.woshipm.com/evaluating/4300714.html , https://sspai.com/post/87587
- 墨墨: self-rated 认识/模糊/忘记; only first rating of the day affects long-term schedule; critics note self-rating lets you overrate. https://www.maimemo.com/help30 , https://www.woshipm.com/evaluating/4300714.html
- Anki/FSRS: FSRS wiki page moved (https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/awesome-fsrs/wiki/ABC-of-FSRS); not re-checked. Anki itself is self-rated. LingQ not researched in time.

## Takeaways used in BRIEF
1. Auto-grade from answers; only first attempt of day hits long-term schedule (墨墨 rule).
2. Same item, harder format as it matures (Clozemaster MC->typing, 百词斩 deep review).
3. Sentence + audio as the unit (PhrasePump, Clozemaster).
4. Target "challenging but attainable" (Birdbrain); response time as Hard signal is our own heuristic, untested.

## Draw order / depth
0 wall, floor (tiled) → 1 wall-mounted (backsplash, window, shelf) → 2 back furniture on backBase (fridge, counter, desk, table, chairs) → 3 items on furniture (laptop, plant, table props) → (pendant hangs on layer 1) → 4 cast + cat on the feet line, sorted by feet y (shadow first) → 5 hand props → 6 front furniture (y > feet line) if a shot needs it → 7 light multiply (room full strength, cast reduced) → bubbles → UI.

## Variants / lighting (follows the approved Soir rule: "night filter in code, never baked")
- **day:** as approved, unchanged (no pendant drawn).
- **evening (`light/golden.json`):** the room is multiplied by a warm low-sun base [0.86,0.78,0.70], with flat stepped darker side bands (3 steps). A long skewed sun patch from the window crosses the wall (×[1.2,1.02,0.72]) and a warm band runs along the floor. The window shows the sunset view; the pendant is off.
- **night (`light/soir.json`):** base = rule cool nudged toward teal, **[0.24,0.31,0.43]** (rule says [0.26,0.29,0.45]; same luminance). This is a documented deviation from art review, recorded in soir.json as `cool_used` + `deviation`. Lifted ×1.25 for phone readability. Lamps use rule warm [0.95,0.70,0.36] ×cap 1.2.
  - `table_pendant`: a trapezoid cone from the shade down to the table top (a flat 0.28 band, narrow at the shade, wide at the table) → a flat full-warm ellipse on the table top → a 2-step ellipse on the floor (0.5 / 0.3) with a sparse 1-px dither only on its outer edge. The table, chairs and anyone at the table get 0.45 warm fill. Bare wall pixels are excluded from the fill. The shade is drawn ungraded as the emitter.
  - `under_counter_strip`: warm strip (under-shelf strip removed). `laptop_glow`: blue, with an emitting screen.
  - moonlight: a pale cool slanted shaft, 2 low-contrast steps (0.32 core / 0.18 edge). The window pane is ungraded.
- **Cast:** same map at reduced strength: 0.25 where the map is below 1 (light cool tint), 0.6 where it is above 1 (warm fill near lamps). Faces stay readable.

## Light compositing (night)
`light/soir.json` now has two layers: `multiply` (`night_multiplier_half.png`, decode ×2) and `add` (`night_add_half.png`, decode ×2, warm additive). The engine draws `out = clamp(src × multiply + add)`: multiply first, then add. The cast uses multiply at 0.25 / 0.6 strength and add at 0.6. Pendant add values: **no in-air glow** (it read as a dither grid / grey tent at 18×), tabletop 90, floor pool 60/32 flat with no dither. Under-shelf strip removed (it read as a grey ledge line on the teal base). Counter strip: 60 on the backsplash/counter top + a flat 18 step above. The multiply also lifts the table cluster toward warm (0.55; wall pixels excluded). Evening stays multiply-only (golden.json).
